# Fantasy Intelligence

Fantasy Intelligence is an AI-powered fantasy football platform designed to assist with draft preparation, live draft decision-making, mock draft simulation, and roster construction.

The platform combines Sleeper integration, draft analytics, roster strategy, simulation, and recommendation engines to help managers make better decisions before, during, and after a fantasy football draft.

---

# Vision

Fantasy Intelligence aims to become a complete fantasy football decision platform by combining:

- Draft preparation
- Live draft assistance
- Mock draft simulation
- Post-draft analysis
- In-season roster management

The long-term goal is to provide intelligent recommendations while maintaining transparency and manager control over final decisions.

# Recommendation Engine

Fantasy Intelligence uses a layered scoring model that includes:

- Player Ranking
- Positional Need
- Tier Risk
- Scarcity
- Strategy Alignment
- League Tendencies
- Opponent Demand
- Monte Carlo Availability
- Expected Value Modeling

These factors are combined to produce a Draft Score used throughout the platform.
