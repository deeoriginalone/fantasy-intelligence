# F3-D.2 FAAB Intelligence
Adds bounded FAAB guidance to verified F3-D.1 candidates. Tiers: WATCH 1%, LOW 3%, MEDIUM 7%, HIGH 12%, CRITICAL 17%. A primary-need match adds 3 points. Recommended percentage is capped at 25%; high range at 30%. Unit bids are calculated only when remaining budget is explicitly supplied. No transaction is submitted.
