Metric explanations
Survivor Win Probability
Model-estimated probability the recommended team wins outright this week (market/Elo/situational blend). Source: market intelligence predictions. Higher is better.
Survivor Evidence Confidence (Evidence Agreement Score)
Agreement across market, Elo, and situational signals. This measures evidence certainty, not team quality or win probability, and is not a percentage chance of being correct. Shown as Unavailable, never a fabricated value, when any of the three underlying signals is missing.
Future Value
Average win probability across a team's strongest remaining scheduled games this season, from verified schedule and market predictions. Unavailable when no supported future prediction evidence exists — never shown as a neutral 50%.
Stability
1 minus the spread between market, Elo, and situational win-probability estimates for this game. Higher means the models agree; lower means they diverge.
Survivor Score
Strategy-weighted blend of current-week win probability, evidence agreement, and (when available) future preservation value. When future evidence or evidence agreement is unavailable, the score is recalculated from the supported components only, using a disclosed reduced-scope formula ("current-week only" or "current-week probability only") rather than substituting a neutral value.
Future Opportunity Cost
The cost of using a team now instead of saving it, derived only from supported future evidence. Shown as Unavailable, not LOW, when future evidence does not exist.

Public pick percentage or leverage data is not available from a supported source and is not displayed.
