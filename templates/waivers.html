How consensus is used: Fresh FantasyPros consensus (via DynastyProcess) orders same-position options and drives the current-week roster comparison. It is an ordinal expert ranking: never converted to points, never compared across positions, and never a FAAB value. Unranked players are never treated as worse. Rest-of-season consensus is used only for bench depth and drop safety, and only while its upstream source date is within 7 days; it never reorders the current-week options. A rest-of-season edge justifies releasing a player only when the published expert ranges do not overlap (every expert rank for the added player is better than every expert rank for the released one); overlapping ranges are a close call, and missing ranges are unverified. Replacement context counts healthy, addable available players at the same position whose expert ranges reach a player's range; it is never converted into points, a value score, or a FAAB amount.{% if waiver_evidence.warnings %} Legacy local ranking inputs remain unverified ({{ waiver_evidence.warnings|join(', ') }}) and are not used.{% endif %}

▶ Data gaps and methodology

Recent Performance, usage evidence, What Changed context, and snap share are informational only and do not change ranking, FAAB, confidence, recommendations, or transaction behavior.

  • Drops are suggested only by the roster decision engine after a fresh same-position rest-of-season comparison and a post-move lineup check (every starting slot, including FLEX, must stay fillable this week and for the season); projection-only drop suggestions are not shown.
  • Snap-share display uses the owner-approved snap_share.evidence.v1 86400-second informational window; stale factual observations may be shown with their supported week, but are not presented as current.
  • Snap-share remains unavailable when identity, publication, source metadata, or snap value is unavailable.
  • Ownership and eligibility are derived from current Sleeper roster and supported-player evidence.
